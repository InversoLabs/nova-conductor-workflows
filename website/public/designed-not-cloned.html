01 / AN ORIGINAL INSTRUMENTDSP + MACHINE LEARNING

An amplifier
with its own voice.

Designed Not Cloned explores a digital amplifier whose sound and response are designed from the ground up.

DSP gives us precise control over the signal. Machine learning opens another way to shape its nonlinear behavior: how it breaks up, how it sustains, and how it responds to your playing.

The aim is an instrument with a character of its own. Something you learn to play, with a response you can feel.

02 / PERFECT DISTORTIONTHE SCIENCE OF CHARACTER

More than
turning up the gain.

Distortion changes the frequency content of a signal. Feed a nonlinear system a 110 Hz tone and it can generate harmonics at 220, 330, 440 Hz, and beyond. Their balance helps give a sound its warmth, edge, density, and texture.

Hz / illustrative harmonic levels

New frequencies. Deliberate character.

Play several notes together and they interact. Nonlinear processing produces additional frequencies related to their sums and differences. This is intermodulation: part of what makes distortion complex, and part of what can make a chord lose definition.

Perfect Distortion investigates how DSP and learned processing can shape that generated spectral energy deliberately—building richness around a note while controlling the interactions between notes.

Machine learning would help determine the nonlinear response. It does not create physical energy from nothing; the processing redistributes signal energy across frequencies.

03 / RESPONSESHAPED BY YOUR PLAYING

Let the playing
change the behavior.

A single note can bloom. A power chord can carry weight. A complex voicing should still have room to speak.

We’re exploring nonlinear processing that responds to dynamics, frequency content, and the recent history of the signal. A hard attack could change how the next moment saturates. A sustained note could develop differently from a dense chord.

The goal is high-gain character with greater note definition and a response that stays connected to the player.
