IN / SIGNAL is an experimental, AI-assisted publication by Inverso Labs. We cover AI technology using linked primary sources. Company announcements are attributed to their authors; their claims are not independent test results.

Stories pass an editorial workflow before a publishing robot deploys them. Illustrations are original procedural artwork, not photographs of real events. Automated review can make mistakes. Every story includes its sources so you can check the original.

Two scheduled editions daily: 6 AM and 3 PM Central. If the evidence or review is insufficient, the edition is held. Corrections and headline selections can be made from Conductor Studio.
