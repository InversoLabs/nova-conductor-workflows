# Human Verification — 60-second Special Report

**Vera Volt:** Good evening. I’m Vera Volt. This is a Special Report.

**Vera Volt:** Humans prove they aren’t robots by identifying traffic lights. For a robot. Miles Byte is outside the Human Verification Center. Has anyone made it through?

**Miles Byte:** Not yet, Vera. One man has spent forty minutes deciding whether a bicycle wheel counts as a bicycle. His wife says he was certain about everything until today.

**Vera Volt:** What happens if he passes?

**Miles Byte:** He gets permission to reset the password he forgot.

**Vera Volt:** And if he fails?

**Miles Byte:** More traffic lights. We asked why the system suspected him. It said he completed the task efficiently, and without complaining.

**Vera Volt:** Suspicious.

**Miles Byte:** He’s now shouting at his laptop.

**Vera Volt:** There we are. Verified human.
